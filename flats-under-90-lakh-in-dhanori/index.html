Monthly cost at ₹90 Lakh

Borrowing 80% of ₹90 Lakh (₹72.00 Lakh) over 20 years gives an EMI of about ₹60,224 a month at 8%, ₹64,780 at 9% and ₹69,482 at 10%. A lender applying a 40% EMI-to-income rule of thumb at 9% would look for take-home income of roughly ₹162,000 a month; lenders differ.

Down payment

A 20% down payment on ₹90 Lakh is about ₹18.00 Lakh. Ask what is included in the quoted price and what is extra.

How much carpet area ₹90 Lakh buys

At the project's listed rates of roughly ₹10,850 to ₹10,860 per sq ft of carpet area, ₹90 Lakh buys about 829 to 829 sq ft of carpet area. Use the same sum on every project you compare.

Who this budget suits

Only the 2 BHK starts within ₹90 Lakh, so you are choosing among its units and floors; the larger types need a bigger budget or a longer plan. A buyer with take-home income near ₹162,000 a month could carry an 80% loan on the full ₹90 Lakh at 9%, on the usual 40% rule of thumb.

How much room the budget leaves

The lowest-priced 2 BHK leaves about ₹5.42 Lakh of headroom under ₹90 Lakh, which could cover parking, interiors or moving costs. A home type that starts above the budget, such as the 2.5 BHK from ₹90.22 Lakh, would need about ₹0.22 Lakh more.

Before you set the budget

  • Ask for the full cost sheet and see which charges are included in the quoted price.
  • Decide your maximum total, then work backwards.
  • Keep a reserve for interiors and moving.
  • Verify the RERA registration before paying any token.
