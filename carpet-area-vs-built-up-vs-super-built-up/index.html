Carpet vs Built-up vs Super Built-up Area: United Athila Dhanori

Why carpet area is the fair way to compare projects.

Updated 2026-11-28

Three areas, three numbers

  • Carpet area: the usable floor area inside the walls.
  • Built-up area: carpet area plus wall thickness and balconies, depending on the definition.
  • Super built-up (saleable) area: built-up area plus a share of common areas such as lobbies and lifts.

Under RERA, sale is defined by carpet area, which is why United Athila's material lists carpet areas.

Why it changes the rate

A 779 sq ft carpet 2 BHK priced at ₹84.58 Lakh works out to about ₹10,860 per sq ft of carpet area. If another project quotes a lower rate on super built-up area, the same flat could cost more per sq ft of carpet area. Always convert to carpet area before you compare.

What to ask

  • Is the quoted rate per sq ft on carpet or saleable area?
  • How much is the loading from carpet to saleable area?
  • Are balconies and terraces counted in the carpet area?
  • What is the RERA carpet area for this exact home?

Check on the floor plan

Ask for a dimensioned plan and measure the main rooms. If the sum of the room areas is far below the stated carpet area, ask where the difference is.

A quick comparison

Imagine two projects each quoting ₹10,000 per sq ft. If one quotes on carpet area and the other on super built-up area, the second can be 25 to 35 percent cheaper per usable sq ft than it looks, or the other way round, depending on its loading. Only a carpet-to-carpet comparison tells you which is cheaper.

What RERA changed

RERA requires developers to sell by carpet area and to state it in the agreement, which makes projects easier to compare than they used to be. Always read the carpet area figure in the agreement itself, not just the brochure.

Mistakes buyers make

  • Comparing a rate per sq ft on saleable area with a rate on carpet area.
  • Ignoring the loading: two projects with the same carpet area can differ by many sq ft of saleable area.
  • Forgetting that balconies and utility areas may or may not be counted.
  • Not asking for the RERA carpet area of the exact unit.

When in doubt, ask for the carpet area of the unit on the agreement and divide the total payable by it. That single number, the total per sq ft of carpet area, is the fairest way to compare any two projects.
