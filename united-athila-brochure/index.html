United Athila Dhanori Brochure

What to ask for and how to check it.

Updated 2026-11-28

Getting the United Athila brochure

Send an enquiry from this site or use the Call and WhatsApp buttons, and the sales team will share the current brochure, cost sheet and floor plans. We do not host a brochure file here because details change with approvals.

What a good brochure should contain

  • Master plan showing towers, amenities and access roads.
  • Typical floor plans with carpet area for each home type.
  • Specifications: flooring, kitchen, bathrooms, doors, windows, lifts.
  • The RERA registration number and possession date.
  • Parking details and what is included in the quoted price.

Why not rely on the brochure alone

Brochures are designed to sell. Renders show the best light, and an amenity list may include items that open in later phases. Treat every claim as a question: where is it on the master plan, when does it open and is it in the price? Keep a copy of the version you were given, with the date, so that you can compare it with the agreement later.

Check it against the paperwork

Compare the brochure's areas and amenities with the RERA registration and the draft agreement, and ask for any difference to be explained in writing.

See also all flat guides and the total cost calculator.

Floor plans and the cost sheet

When you enquire, ask for the floor plan of the exact home type you want, not just the typical plan, and for the cost sheet for the unit and floor. Compare the carpet area on the plan with the figure on the price list. Differences between the brochure, the price list and the RERA registration are worth raising before you pay anything.

Common mistakes with brochures

  • Treating an artist's impression as a promise: a render can differ from what is built.
  • Missing the fine print on the price page, where inclusions and exclusions are listed.
  • Assuming every amenity opens at possession when some arrive in later phases.
  • Not asking for the version date, so a later "updated" brochure cannot be compared.

A short note of what was promised verbally, with the date and the name of the person, saves arguments later.
