United Athila Dhanori United Athila floor plans and carpet areas

Carpet areas by type, and the master and tower plans available for Towers G, H, I and J.

2 min readUpdated 2026-11-26United Athila, Dhanori

Key takeaways

  • 2 BHK: 779 to 843 sq ft carpet. 2.5 BHK: 806 to 899 sq ft. 3 BHK: 918 to 1,059 sq ft.
  • The project has four towers (G, H, I and J) on a 4.25 acre land parcel.
  • Master layouts and tower floor plans are on the home page.
  • Plans are indicative and subject to final sanctioned plans.

United Athila, Dhanori has 700+ planned homes and shops across four towers on 4.25 acres. Here is how the carpet areas break down and where to find the plans.

Carpet area by configuration

TypeCarpet area
2 BHK779 – 843 sq ft
2.5 BHK806 – 899 sq ft
3 BHK918 – 1,059 sq ft

Carpet area is the usable area inside the flat, which is why it is the best number to compare between projects. The cost sheet also shows a larger figure that includes a share of common areas, so check which number a price is based on. See the United Athila price list for both figures.

Plans available

  • Master layouts: ground floor and first floor.
  • Tower G: upper ground, first, typical and 7th (refuge) floor.
  • Tower H: 9th to 11th floor.
  • Tower I: typical and 7th (refuge) floor.
  • Tower J: first, typical and 7th (refuge) floor.

You can open and enlarge each plan in the floor plans section on the home page.

What to look for in a plan

  1. Is the living room wide enough for your furniture, and does it get light?
  2. Are the bedrooms and bathrooms placed so that doors do not clash?
  3. Where is the kitchen, and is there a utility area?
  4. How many flats share each lift lobby on the typical floor?

Ask the sales team which tower and floor each carpet area is in. Plans are indicative and subject to final sanctioned plans.
