How to verify the registration

  1. Open the official MahaRERA website.
  2. Go to the registered projects search and enter the registration number PM1260002602534.
  3. Check the project name, the developer, the address and the dates shown for the project.
  4. Match the registration number with the one printed on the agreement and the brochure.

Why it matters

A RERA-registered project has its approvals, timelines and details on public record. The project page also lists the possession timeline that the developer has declared, so read it there and confirm it with the sales team in writing.

How to verify the registration yourself

You do not have to take our word for it. Open the MahaRERA website, search for the registration number PM1260002602534 and compare what you see with what you were told. The registration page shows the promoter, the land details, the proposed completion date and the quarterly progress updates the promoter has filed.

  1. Confirm the project name and promoter match the sales material.
  2. Note the proposed completion date, and any extension recorded later.
  3. Look at the filed progress updates and compare them with what you see on site.
  4. Check the complaints section for the project and the promoter.

What RERA gives you

RERA requires carpet area to be the basis of sale, requires the promoter to declare a completion date, and gives buyers a way to complain if the date is missed. Read the draft agreement for the clauses on possession, delays and cancellation before you pay more than a token.
