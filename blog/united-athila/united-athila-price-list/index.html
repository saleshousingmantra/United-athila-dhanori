United Athila Dhanori United Athila price list: 2, 2.5 and 3 BHK in Dhanori

Unit-wise indicative prices for United Athila, Dhanori, with stamp duty, registration and GST.

2 min readUpdated 2026-11-26United Athila, Dhanori

Key takeaways

  • 2 BHK starts at ₹84.58 lakh, 2.5 BHK at ₹90.22 lakh and 3 BHK at ₹99.63 lakh (all-inclusive, per the current cost sheet).
  • Prices include stamp duty at 7%, registration of ₹30,000 and GST at 5%.
  • Carpet areas range from 779 sq ft (2 BHK) to 1,059 sq ft (3 BHK).
  • Prices are indicative and can change, so confirm the latest cost sheet with the sales team.
