Living in Dhanori, Pune: a locality overview

Where Dhanori is in East Pune, what is close by and how to check it yourself on Google Maps.

2 min readUpdated 2026-11-26United Athila, Dhanori

Key takeaways

  • Dhanori is a residential locality in the north-east of Pune, near Viman Nagar, Lohegaon and Yerwada.
  • Public listings put Pune Airport roughly 4 to 5 km away, and Ramwadi is the nearest operating metro station.
  • Pune Railway Station is approximately 9 km from the project.
  • Use the Google Maps links to check live distance and travel time.

Dhanori is a residential locality in the north-east of Pune. It sits close to Viman Nagar, Lohegaon, Yerwada, Vishrantwadi and Kalyani Nagar. United Athila, by United Landmarks LLP, is at Dhanori Central, Survey No. 6, Hissa No. 2, Dhanori, Pune 411015.

Where Dhanori is

The project is reached by 24 m and 30 m wide roads. Well-known landmarks nearby include Dhanori Jakat Naka, Porwal Road, Goodwill Square, Dhanori Road, Dhanori–Lohegaon Road and the Pune Airport Road.
