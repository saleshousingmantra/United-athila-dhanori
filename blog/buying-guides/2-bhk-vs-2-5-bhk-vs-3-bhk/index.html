United Athila Dhanori 2 BHK vs 2.5 BHK vs 3 BHK: which fits you

Carpet areas, starting prices and who each layout suits, with numbers from United Athila, Dhanori.

2 min readUpdated 2026-11-26United Athila, Dhanori

Key takeaways

  • United Athila offers 2 BHK (779–843 sq ft carpet), 2.5 BHK (806–899) and 3 BHK (918–1,059).
  • All-inclusive prices start around ₹84.58 L, ₹90.22 L and ₹99.63 L respectively, as per the current cost sheet.
  • A 2.5 BHK adds a flexible room for work, a nursery or a guest.
  • Choose by household size in five years, a comfortable EMI and how you will use the extra room.

United Athila, by United Landmarks LLP, offers three home types at Dhanori Central in East Pune. Picking between them is mostly a question of space, budget and how long you plan to stay. Here are the numbers and a simple way to decide.

The three layouts at a glance

2 BHK2.5 BHK3 BHK

All-inclusive prices include stamp duty, registration and GST as per the current cost sheet, and are subject to change.

Who suits a 2 BHK

  • Couples and small families who want a well-planned home without paying for unused rooms.
  • First-time buyers who want a lower loan amount and EMI.
  • Investors planning to rent the home out.

What the half bedroom in a 2.5 BHK is for

A 2.5 BHK adds a smaller room to the two bedrooms. Families use it as a home office, a nursery, a study or a guest room. It suits buyers who want more flexibility than a 2 BHK without the budget of a full 3 BHK.

Who suits a 3 BHK

  • Families with children or parents living together who need a room for each.
  • Professionals who work from home and need a dedicated study.
  • Buyers planning to stay long term, because a third bedroom grows with the family.

A simple way to decide

  1. List how many people will live in the home five years from now.
  2. Set a comfortable EMI, then see which price band fits.
  3. Decide whether you need a separate room for work or guests.
  4. Compare the floor plans of the exact unit you are considering.
