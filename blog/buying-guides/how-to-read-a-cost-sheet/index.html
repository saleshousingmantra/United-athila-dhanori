United Athila Dhanori How to read a cost sheet: stamp duty, registration and GST

How agreement value, stamp duty, registration and GST add up to the all-inclusive price.

2 min readUpdated 2026-11-26United Athila, Dhanori

Key takeaways

  • The all-inclusive price is the agreement value plus stamp duty, registration and GST.
  • United Athila's current cost sheet uses stamp duty at 7%, registration of ₹30,000 and GST at 5%.
  • Example: an agreement value of ₹75,25,000 gives a total of ₹84,58,000.
  • Rates and charges can change, so confirm the latest cost sheet in writing.

A cost sheet breaks the price of a flat into its parts. Reading it properly helps you compare projects fairly and plan your budget. Here is how the pieces fit together, using the current cost sheet for United Athila, Dhanori.

The parts of a cost sheet

  • Agreement value: the price of the flat as written in your agreement.
  • Stamp duty: a government charge on the agreement. The current cost sheet uses 7%.
  • Registration: a fixed registration charge. The current cost sheet shows ₹30,000.
  • GST: shown at 5% in the current cost sheet.

A worked example

ItemAmount
Agreement value (2 BHK, 779 sq ft carpet)₹75,25,000
Stamp duty (7%)₹5,26,750
Registration₹30,000
GST (5%)₹3,76,250
All-inclusive total₹84,58,000

These figures are from the indicative cost sheet for the 779 sq ft 2 BHK. Larger units follow the same method with a higher agreement value.

What to check

  1. Is the price all-inclusive of stamp duty, registration and GST?
  2. Are maintenance, parking and club charges included or extra?
  3. Is the carpet area stated, and does it match the plan?
  4. Is the payment schedule written in the agreement?

Prices are indicative and subject to change as per the agreement. Read more about pricing stages in our guide to pre-launch and early-bird pricing.

A worked example from the cost sheet

Take the first 2 BHK on the United Athila cost sheet, with 779 sq ft of carpet area. The agreement value is ₹75,25,000. Stamp duty at 7% adds ₹5,26,750 and GST at 5% adds ₹3,76,250. With registration of ₹30,000, the all-inclusive total is ₹84,58,000. In other words, taxes and registration add about ₹9.33 lakh, roughly 12% on top of the agreement value. If a different project quotes only the agreement value, add about 12% before you compare.

What to ask about every line

  • Is the quoted price per sq ft on carpet or saleable area?
  • Are floor rise, parking and club charges included or extra?
  • Which taxes are included, and at what rate?
  • What is the payment schedule, and what triggers each instalment?
