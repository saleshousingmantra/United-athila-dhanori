What is pre-launch and early-bird pricing

What pre-launch means, how early-bird offers work and what to verify before you pay.

2 min readUpdated 2026-11-26United Athila, Dhanori

Key takeaways

  • Pre-launch means a project is offered to early buyers before the formal launch.
  • Early-bird pricing is an introductory price that can change as the project moves forward.
  • Always verify the MahaRERA registration (United Athila: PM1260002602534) on the official website.
  • Get the cost sheet, payment terms and possession timeline in writing.

When a new project is called "pre-launch", it is being offered to a limited group of buyers before the full public launch. United Athila, Dhanori, is currently in pre-launch. This guide explains what that means for you.

What pre-launch means

A pre-launch lets buyers register interest and choose units early. Plans, specifications and prices are indicative and can change as approvals and sales progress. That is why every pre-launch price carries a note that it is subject to change.

How early-bird pricing works

An early-bird price is an introductory rate offered to the first buyers. It is usually available for a limited time or for a limited number of units. Always ask what exactly is covered, how long the offer lasts and what happens to the price afterwards.

Check the RERA registration first

In Maharashtra, projects must be registered with MahaRERA. United Athila's registration number is PM1260002602534. You can verify any project on the official site, maharera.maharashtra.gov.in, before paying anything.

Questions to ask before you book

  • What is included in the all-inclusive price, and what is extra?
  • What is the payment schedule?
  • What is the expected possession timeline?
  • What are the terms if you decide to cancel?
