United Athila Dhanori Reviews

How to judge the project and developer yourself.

Updated 2026-11-28

An honest note on reviews

We publish no reviews or testimonials that we cannot verify. Here is how to form your own view of United Athila and United Landmarks LLP.

How to evaluate the project

  1. Ask for a list of completed United Landmarks LLP projects and visit one. Talk to residents about build quality, maintenance and handover.
  2. Look up the project and the developer on MahaRERA, including any complaints.
  3. Read the agreement draft and ask a property lawyer to review it.
  4. Compare the total cost sheet with two other projects in Dhanori.
  5. Visit the site and the sales gallery, and check the access road.

Good signs and red flags

Good signs: a RERA number you can verify, a clear cost sheet, willingness to put promises in writing. Red flags: pressure to pay a large amount before the agreement, vague possession dates, refusing to share the draft agreement.

Questions to put to the developer

  • How many projects has United Landmarks LLP delivered, and where?
  • Were they delivered on the declared date?
  • Who maintains the buildings after handover, and for how long?
  • Have there been any legal disputes or complaints on any project?

See also how to verify the possession date.

Talk to people who live there

The most useful review of a developer is a conversation with residents of a completed project. Ask about build quality in the first two years, how quickly defects were fixed, how maintenance is run and whether promised amenities exist. Visit on a weekend evening, when residents are home and the common areas are in use.

Check the paperwork, not the promises

Reviews can be written by anyone, so lean on documents. The RERA registration shows the promoter, the land and the declared dates. The draft agreement shows what you are really buying and what happens if either side fails. A cost sheet with every charge listed shows whether the headline price is honest. If a seller is uncomfortable with you checking these, that is information too.

Compare before you decide

Put two other projects in the same area next to this one and compare carpet area, total payable, declared possession date and the developer's record. The right project is the one that wins on the facts you can verify, not the one with the best brochure.
