A rough affordability check

A common rule of thumb keeps total loan EMIs below about 40% of monthly take-home income, though lenders differ. On that basis, an EMI of about ₹64,942 (80% of the lowest listed 2.5 BHK price, 9% a year, 20 years) points to a household take-home income of roughly ₹162,000 a month. Treat it as a conversation starter with your bank.
