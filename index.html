Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is United Athila, Dhanori located?

At Dhanori Central, East Pune, next to the upcoming 108-acre "Dhanori Central" development, with access via 24 m and 30 m wide roads.

What configurations are available?

2 BHK (779 to 843 sq.ft.), 2.5 BHK (806 to 899 sq.ft.) and 3 BHK (918 to 1,059 sq.ft.) residences, plus select commercial inventory.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. The MahaRERA registration number is PM1260002602534. You can verify it on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

How well connected is it?

Close to Pune Airport, easy access to Ramwadi Metro Station, Pune Railway Station approximately 9 km away, and convenient access to major IT and employment hubs.
