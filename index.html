Project Overview

United Athila is an upcoming premium residential and commercial development by United Landmarks LLP at Dhanori Central, East Pune, on a 4.25 acre land parcel with 700+ planned homes and shops across Towers G, H, I & J.

It sits next to the upcoming 108-acre Dhanori Central development, with quick access to Pune Airport, Ramwadi Metro and major IT hubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is United Athila, Dhanori located?

At Survey No. 6, Hissa No. 2, Dhanori, Pune 411015, at Dhanori Central next to the upcoming 108-acre "Dhanori Central" development, with access via 24 m and 30 m wide roads.

What are the nearby landmarks?

Dhanori Jakat Naka, Porwal Road, Goodwill Square, Dhanori Road, Dhanori–Lohegaon Road, Lohegaon, Viman Nagar and Pune Airport Road are all close by.

What configurations are available?

2 BHK (779 to 843 sq.ft.), 2.5 BHK (806 to 899 sq.ft.) and 3 BHK (918 to 1,059 sq.ft.) residences, plus select commercial inventory.

Is the project RERA registered?

Yes. The MahaRERA registration number is PM1260002602534. You can verify it on maharera.maharashtra.gov.in.

How well connected is it?

Close to Pune Airport, easy access to Ramwadi Metro Station, Pune Railway Station approximately 9 km away, and convenient access to major IT and employment hubs.
