Buyer guide

United Athila Dhanori Site Visit

A checklist for the sales gallery and the site.

Updated 2026-11-28

Before you go

Book a visit through the enquiry form or the Call and WhatsApp buttons. Take your ID, your budget and a printed list of questions, and bring a family member who will help decide.

At the sales gallery

  • See the model flat and note it is a sample, not the final fit-out.
  • Ask for the cost sheet for the exact home and floor you want.
  • Ask for the RERA number, floor plans and draft agreement.
  • Ask about token terms and cancellation terms.

At the site

  • Check the access road and how it joins the main roads.
  • Look at the surroundings of the tower you prefer.
  • Ask where amenities and parking will be.
  • Check the state of construction of the tower you are considering.

Take notes and photographs

Photograph every page of the cost sheet you are shown and note the name of the person who gave it. Write down any promise made verbally, then ask for it in writing. Visit a second time without the sales team, at a different hour.

Then test your budget on the EMI calculator.

Bring the people who will live there

Parents, partners and older children notice different things: the walk from the lift, the light in the bedroom, the noise at night. Ask each person to list two things they like and two they would change, and compare the lists on the way home.

Ten questions to take with you

  1. What is the total payable for my unit, line by line?
  2. What is the declared possession date for my tower?
  3. Which amenities are ready at handover?
  4. How many parking spaces are included?
  5. What are the maintenance charges and what do they cover?
  6. What are the payment stages and their triggers?
  7. Can I see the draft agreement today?
  8. What happens if I cancel after the token?
  9. Who will maintain the buildings after handover?
  10. Can you put the key promises in writing?
