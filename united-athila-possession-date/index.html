United Athila Dhanori Possession Date

How to verify the declared date and what to ask about delays.

Updated 2026-11-28

What matters about possession

The date that matters is the one declared in the project's RERA registration. The project is registered under MahaRERA PM1260002602534; verify it on the MahaRERA website before paying any token. Marketing phrases such as "possession soon" are not binding.

How to find the declared date

  1. Search the project on the MahaRERA website using its registration number.
  2. Open the project details and note the proposed completion date.
  3. Check for any extension or revision recorded after registration.

Ask about delays

  • What compensation or interest applies if possession is late, as per the agreement?
  • Will possession be tower-wise, and when does your tower complete?
  • Which amenities will be ready at handover?
  • When is the occupation certificate expected?

Plan your finances around the date

Until possession you may pay rent and, once disbursements begin, pre-EMI interest. A delay of a year can cost a family a large sum, so build a buffer into your plan and prefer construction-linked payment schedules that follow progress you can see.

See also the EMI calculator.

Why the declared date matters

The date in the RERA registration is the one the developer commits to in law. Compare it with the stage of construction you see on site, and with how earlier projects by the same developer performed against their own declared dates. A project that is far from the declared stage on a site visit deserves a direct question in writing.

Read the agreement clause

The agreement for sale usually states the possession date and what happens if it is missed. Read that clause, and the clauses on force majeure and on changes to the plan, before you sign. If a clause is unclear, ask a property lawyer to explain it.

Questions to put in writing

  1. What is the declared possession date for my tower, and has it been revised?
  2. What is the stage of construction today, and what is the next milestone?
  3. What happens to my payments if the date is missed?
  4. Who confirms the occupation certificate, and when do you expect it?

Keep the written answers with your booking papers. If the date matters to you, for example because your lease ends or a child starts school, say so before you pay, and ask for a payment plan that follows construction stages.
